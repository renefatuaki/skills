The progress band shows the weekly Claude limits for an account with no 5-hour window, such as `Claude week 67% · Fable 57%` on the Ultra plan, the weekly limit and the weekly limit of each model. An account with a 5-hour window keeps `Claude 5h 77%`. The Claude usage stands at the right edge as the first of the usages, before the Codex and the Antigravity usage, instead of at the left edge before the progress.
