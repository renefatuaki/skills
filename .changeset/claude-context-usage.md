The progress band shows the share of the context window the session has used as the first reading of the Claude usage, such as `Claude Context 34% · 5h 77%`. The other readings show the share left, this one shows the share used. With no context figure yet, in a fresh session or just after a compaction, the reading is left out. Off a subscription the usage reads `Claude Context 34%`.
