Open decisions at the end of a reply now go out as AskUserQuestion questions in the same turn. In about 19 sessions a reply ended with its open decisions as a prose list and the user had to ask for them interactively, and several questions were rejected because a term went unexplained or an option claimed a number no measurement showed. Writing the reply in the engineering core asks every open decision, grilling rounds included, through the tool with one decision per question, the recommended option first, the facts as text before it, one plain sentence per option, explained terms and only measured numbers. The Ticket playbook asks its questions and its grilling rounds the same way.
